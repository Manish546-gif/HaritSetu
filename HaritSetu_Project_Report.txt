HARITSETU: THE DECENTRALIZED INFRASTRUCTURE FOR SUSTAINABLE AGRO-ECOLOGY
Project ID: HSETU-2026-ALPHA
Date: February 26, 2026

================================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================
HaritSetu ("The Green Bridge") is a decentralized, Web3-powered platform 
designed to transform environmental stewardship into a tangible financial asset. 
By bridging the gap between smallholder farmers and industrial emitters using 
blockchain-verified data, HaritSetu creates a high-integrity voluntary carbon 
market (VCM) that rewards green deeds directly.

================================================================================
2. THE PROBLEM
================================================================================
The current carbon credit market is broken:
- Intermediaries absorb up to 80% of the financial value.
- Small farmers are excluded due to high verification costs.
- Lack of precision data leads to "greenwashing" and double-counting.

================================================================================
3. FUNCTIONAL WORKFLOW (THE CIRCULAR ECONOMY)
================================================================================

3.1 FOR FARMERS (SUPPLY SIDE - THE MINT)
----------------------------------------
1. GIS MAPPING: Farmers draw their land polygons directly on our map.
2. CALCULATOR: The system measures hectares and applies crop-specific 
   sequestration coefficients.
3. GREEN MULTIPLIERS: 1.5x bonus for using EVs and green machinery.
4. VERIFICATION: Admin panel reviews GIS data against satellite NDVI imagery.
5. TOKEN ISSUANCE: HSETU tokens are minted directly to the farmer's wallet.

3.2 FOR BUSINESSES (DEMAND SIDE - THE BURN)
-------------------------------------------
1. COMPLIANCE TRACKING: Real-time monitoring of industrial CO2 footprint.
2. DYNAMIC BURNING: Credits are automatically "burned" from the business 
   wallet as they emit CO2.
3. MARKETPLACE: Businesses buy HSETU tokens directly from farmers to 
   maintain compliance status.

================================================================================
4. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================
- Frontend: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS.
- 3D Visualization: Three.js (The EcoGlobe).
- GIS Engine: Leaflet.js (Precision mapping).
- Blockchain Bridge: Ethers.js for immutable ledger synchronization.
