\section{Introduction: The Vision of HaritSetu}
\subsection{1.1 Philosophical Foundation}
HaritSetu (meaning \textit{"The Green Bridge"}) is built on the belief that environmental stewardship should be the most profitable activity on Earth. For centuries, industrial growth has been decoupled from ecological health. HaritSetu aims to re-attach these concepts using decentralized ledgers, ensuring that every molecule of $CO_2$ sequestered by a farmer is a tangible asset in the global economy.

\subsection{1.2 The Core Objectives}
The primary objectives of the HaritSetu protocol are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Democratization}: Lower the barrier to entry for smallholder farmers (SHFs) from thousands of dollars to literally zero.
    \item \textbf{High-Integrity Credits}: Eliminate "Greenwashing" by linking carbon credits to specific, verifiable GPS coordinates and real-time biological data.
    \item \textbf{Active Accountability}: Shift corporate carbon offsetting from a passive annual purchase to a real-time, dynamic "burn" process linked to industrial output.
\end{itemize}

\section{Detailed Problem Statement: The Broken VCM}
\subsection{2.1 Economic Inefficiency (The Value Leak)}
The current Voluntary Carbon Market (VCM) is a fragmented ecosystem of intermediaries. Typically, when a corporation pays \$30 for a carbon credit, the distribution looks as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Brokers \& Resellers}: 35\% (\$10.50)
    \item \textbf{Third-Party Auditors}: 25\% (\$7.50)
    \item \textbf{Platform Registries}: 15\% (\$4.50)
    \item \textbf{Project Developer}: 15\% (\$4.50)
    \item \textbf{The Farmer/Sequester}: \textbf{10\% (\$3.00)}
\end{itemize}
HaritSetu aims to invert this pyramid.

\section{Functional Flow: The HaritSetu Lifecycle}
\subsection{3.1 Supply Side: Carbon Sequestration (Minting)}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Mapping & Survey}: The farmer logs into the dashboard and uses the GIS interface to define the field's boundary.
    \item \textbf{Calculation}: Based on the area (calculated via Haversine formula) and the selected crop type (Sequestration Coefficient), the system determines the potential credits.
    \item \textbf{Green Multipliers}: If the farmer proves the use of EV tractors or green machinery, a 1.5x multiplier is applied.
    \item \textbf{Admin Approval}: Our admin panel reviews the satellite NDVI data to verify the existence of the bio-mass.
    \item \textbf{HSETU Issuance}: Tokens are minted directly to the farmer's Web3 wallet.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{3.2 Demand Side: Industrial Compliance (Burning)}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Baseline Allocation}: Businesses are allocated (or purchase) a baseline of credits according to their production scale.
    \item \textbf{Live Emission Tracking}: An industrial dashboard simulates/reports actual $CO_2$ emissions.
    \item \textbf{Automated Burn}: As emission occurs, HSETU tokens are permanently "burned" from the business wallet.
    \item \textbf{Compliance Gap}: When credits drop below 10\%, the business receives a "Non-Compliant" warning and must buy from the P2P marketplace.
\end{enumerate}
